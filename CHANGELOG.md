# Changelog

Every version of Ripasso since v7.2 (the known-good starting point), newest first, one line each. On GitHub each version is marked by a tag, `v` and its number; from 9.1 the tag is added automatically when the version reaches `main`.

- **9.31** (2026-11-25): Verbs sessions are balanced like the grammar units (planSession): the drill for each verb tense is chosen from a table (pickKey) with the same chances as before, so a session can steer it kind for kind; each verb tense gets a different kind from the one it was last answered with on this device (itapp.lasttype.v1, now shared by grammar items and verb tenses, its cap raised from 2,000 to 4,000), a session has at least four kinds in 12 exercises where its items allow, and no kind three times running. A verb tense missed 4 times or more and not yet past box 3 starts again with its New tense card, then a table. Focus sessions on the Verbs reference: Practise this verb (in each opened row), Practise a tense (a chip per tense, with how many verbs have it started) and Practise this family (under each family's heading), each taking what is due, then the rest started, least known first, then new tenses that can open, never one that hasn't; the summary names the focus. Weak spots: every verb form missed (a wrong table cell, or a wrong answer to a drill on one form) is remembered on the device in itapp.vgmiss.v1 (at most 1,500 cells, never synced or backed up), a later right answer healing it; Stats has a Verbs: weak spots card (misses by person and by tense, and the 8 cells missed most) with Practise my weak spots, whose tables ask only the persons missed; elsewhere a drill on one form asks for a missed person about half the time.
- **9.30** (2026-11-22): A new grammar unit, The passive and the causative (u22, order 16.25, between the longer sentences unit and the slang unit). Part one: the passive with essere in every tense, with da for the doer; venire for an action, in the simple tenses only (la porta viene chiusa beside la porta è chiusa); andare for must (va fatto) and andare perso; the passive with the modals, as an infinitive and in the subjunctive; only a direct object as the subject (mi è stato dato un libro); the passive si in every tense and with the modals; what speech says instead (mi hanno rubato la bici, il libro l'ha scritto Marco); headlines and notices; and, to recognise, fu costruito and rimanere ferito. Part two: fare + infinitive (make, let, have done), who does it (a direct object, a or da), the pronouns with fare (la faccio riparare, fammi vedere), the compound tenses (l'ho fatta riparare), farsi (mi sono fatta tagliare i capelli), far vedere, far sapere and the other everyday pairs, lasciare + infinitive and lasciare che, and seeing and hearing + infinitive (l'ho visto uscire). A 20-section lesson with a news report and notices and a garage and hairdresser dialogue, and 194 items (23 words, the preposition da shared with the prepositions unit, 53 sentences, 45 transforms, 19 grammar questions, 16 spot the mistake, 10 endings, 11 replies, 8 sorts, 8 matches). The prepositions unit's da now lists by (after a passive), and the longer sentences unit's question on si vendono libri calls it the passive si, as its lesson does. The lessons of the tenses, irregular verbs, prepositions, subjunctive, compound tenses, pronouns, register, longer sentences and slang units link to the new unit; the wild card has a (Passive & causative) tag and five items that use it. tools/test-choices.js counted two remembered mix-ups whose English reads the same (goodbye, goodbye (very formal)) as two options the meaning choice should offer; the app rightly offers one, and the check now counts them once, with a fault for it in tools/test-the-tests.js. Content guide v3.25.

The agreement drill took a noun's plural gender from its singular, so a noun like il paio (le paia) or l'uovo (le uova) would have asked for le paia diversi; it now takes it from the plural article, and tools/test-rules.js checks it, with a fault for it in tools/test-the-tests.js. - **9.26** (2026-11-15): Saved data moves from localStorage to IndexedDB, behind one storage script (`Store`, in `<head>`): every read and write of progress, packs and the per-device values goes through it, and nothing else in the page names the browser's storage (`check-content.js` checks). Progress is kept in parts (a head, the daily log by year, the records in 256 buckets), so an answer writes one small bucket instead of the whole copy: saving one answer at the worst case, with the CPU slowed 4×, takes about 35 ms instead of about 180. On first opening, every `itapp.*` key is copied in one transaction, read back and compared with the original, and only then is the move marked done; closed at any point before, the next start does it all again from the old copy, which is left untouched and never written again. Where IndexedDB can't be used and the data has never been moved, the app saves in localStorage as before and says so on Today and in Your data; after the move it never goes back to the old copy (IndexedDB unavailable, emptied or refusing a save is said plainly instead). Saves are committed as each answer is given and everything is compared and written as the app is put away or closed; two windows on one storage (the installed app and a browser tab) merge each other's saves, the newest record of each item winning, and the offline worker now reloads every open window when an update is applied. Your data has a Storage card (which storage, how much is saved, the room left, when the data was moved, and Merge in the copy from before the move); an older version saving to the old copy afterwards is noticed and offered for merging. New `tools/test-storage.js` (145 checks: the move, an interrupted move, the fallbacks, a crash after a save, two windows, updating from and going back to 9.25); the progress test's room sections now measure IndexedDB and the cloud documents, which set the room from here on (about 18,000 more records); `docs/storage-room.md` is rewritten, and its option 3 (packed records) is no longer needed.
