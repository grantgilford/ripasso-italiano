# Changelog

Every version of Ripasso since v7.2 (the known-good starting point), newest first, one line each. On GitHub each version is marked by a tag, `v` and its number; from 9.1 the tag is added automatically when the version reaches `main`.

- **9.18** (2026-10-27): A new Vocab pack 9 (unit fc10) of 1,000 cards between packs 8 and 10, median frequency rank 8,592 (pack 8: 6,689; the old pack 9: 10,232): 442 idioms, sayings and spoken expressions built from everyday words (*prendere sottogamba*, *tagliare la testa al toro*, *chi tace acconsente*) and 558 single words: everyday things and people (*coinquilino*, *guinzaglio*, *scorciatoia*), feelings and character, colloquial Italian (*fregatura*, *gufare*), and 72 words of the news and public life that look like their English twin, each with the spelling to watch. No card repeats a word from another pack, a grammar unit or the Verbs section, or a word an earlier card's note teaches. The old pack 9 (fc05) becomes pack 10; unit and item ids are unchanged, so progress is untouched. `tools/pack-levels.js` now counts words cut short (*far*, *ciel*, *caval*), plurals such as *bianche* and *gocce*, and *uomini* as their dictionary words, and leaves out the remaining joined forms of *di*, *a*, *da*, *in* and *su* (*degli*, *nei*, *sui*…), so three medians moved slightly (pack 4: 1,720, was 1,737; pack 7: 5,549, was 5,653; the old pack 9: 10,232, was 10,316). New `docs/storage-room.md`: how much room the browser copy of progress has left (2.95 MB of the progress test's 3 MB margin with every item practised, against the browser's 5 MiB), where the space goes and the options for more; the progress test now prints how many more records fit.
- **9.17** (2026-10-27): A new Vocab pack 7 (unit fc09) of 1,000 cards between packs 6 and 8, median frequency rank 5,653 (pack 6: 4,348; the old pack 7: 6,689): 352 idioms and spoken expressions built from everyday words (*mettere in chiaro*, *venire al dunque*, *vedersela*) and 648 single words, mostly ones a learner can't guess from English (everyday actions and things at home such as *tossire*, *sbucciare* and *sturare*, character, and the formal Italian of the news and official forms). No card repeats a word from another pack, a grammar unit or the Verbs section, or a word an earlier card's note teaches, and every first English gloss another card shares is a true synonym or limited by a bracket. The old packs 7 and 8 (fc08, fc05) become packs 8 and 9; unit and item ids are unchanged, so progress is untouched.
- **9.16** (2026-10-25): The answer panel shows what the Italian means, right or wrong, wherever the exercise showed Italian without its English and the English isn't what it asks for, so it is never a hint: the verb of a conjugation (*sognare: to dream*), the noun of an article or plural drill, the adjective and noun of an agreement drill, the preposition and noun of a merged form, the verb of the Verbs section's Which verb?, Which person?, Which tense? and Change the tense, and the sentence of Listen and type (before, only after a mistake). The 407 transforms gain `en`, the English of their first answer (also on the New pattern card and in the glossary), and the 242 replies `lineEn`, the English of the other person's line; the content check now asks for both. The choices test checks every exercise of every item (218,100) shows its English, asks for it or gives it in the panel, and the panel on the screen.

- **9.8** (2026-10-23): Variety pass for Irregular verbs (u04) and Verb tenses (u05): each gains the five practice types added in v8.6 (24 spot, 14 endings, 12 reply, 7 sort and 7 match items), aimed at the rules its lesson teaches, and goes from 7 kinds of exercise to 12 (the commonest kind from 23% to 17% in u04, from 31% to 26% in u05). - **9.7** (2026-10-23): Wrong options and the answer panel teach more, built only from forms the content or VERB_GYM states. Word order: one of the two extra words is a near-miss where the data allows (another person of the sentence's verb in the same tense, the other article of the same number, another form of an adjective), never one that makes a sentence the item accepts, and the answer panel names it with its reason. Meaning choices: words with the same part of speech and gender first, then look-alikes, one option per word, and the whole course searched only when the unit runs short (about 17 times faster). Confusion memory: a wrong answer that is another word's right answer is remembered on the device (itapp.confusions.v1, never synced, at most 500 pairs), that word is offered first next time, and from the second time the panel says "You've mixed up X and Y N times". Flashcards: after each answer the panel shows a sentence from the grammar units that uses the card's word (661 cards have one), with its English and a speaker. New check: tools/test-choices.js.
- **9.6** (2026-10-23): Practice joined to the lessons. Every item of a unit with a lesson is linked to the section that teaches it (`ITEM_SECTIONS`, proposed by the new `tools/link-sections.js` and reviewed by hand; 43 items left unlinked on purpose, 12 linked to another unit's section). After a wrong answer, and on a new item's first exercise, the answer panel offers Read the rule, which opens that section in a sheet over the session without changing it; each lesson section with linked items ends with Practise this section; Continue and unit sessions take new items in the order of the lesson's sections. The content check, add-items and edit-items handle the links; new checks in test-ui, test-sessions and test-speedups.
- **9.5** (2026-10-23): Grammar sessions (Continue, a unit, Review, Wild card, Mistakes, most-missed) are put together with the whole session in view: once the items are picked, each one's exercise is chosen so that no kind comes three times in a row, a 12-exercise session has at least four kinds where its items allow, and the share of typed exercises stays as it was (a hint to makeExercise, which only chooses between exercises of the same kind); each device remembers the kind each grammar item was last shown with (itapp.lasttype.v1, never synced) and prefers another next time; a correct answer that schedules a review at box 3 or above moves it by up to 10% either way; an item with 4 or more lapses at box 3 or below is tricky, and starts again with its introduction and then the usual typed follow-up, Stats listing up to 15; Stats shows how many grammar items, cards and verbs fall due on each of the next 7 days; and the summary offers Another round. Boxes, intervals, promotion and every storage format are as before. New check: tools/test-sessions.js.
- **9.4** (2026-10-21): Cheaper cloud sync and sturdier progress, with no storage format changed: after an answer only the documents that changed (its record's shard, the year's daily log and meta) are built and compared, with every document compared on loading, after a backup or a reset and when the app is put away; a snapshot from the cloud merges only the documents new since the last one, record by record, without building the content again; and every progress record is checked as it is loaded, unpacked from the cloud, merged or read from a backup, a damaged one repaired or left out (loading a backup says how many). New checks: room for a record for every item, damaged records, writes limited to what changed, and the merge against 9.3's.
