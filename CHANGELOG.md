# Changelog

Every version of Ripasso since v7.2 (the known-good starting point), newest first, one line each. On GitHub each version is marked by a tag, `v` and its number; from 9.1 the tag is added automatically when the version reaches `main`.

- **9.5** (2026-10-23): Grammar sessions (Continue, a unit, Review, Wild card, Mistakes, most-missed) are put together with the whole session in view: once the items are picked, each one's exercise is chosen so that no kind comes three times in a row, a 12-exercise session has at least four kinds where its items allow, and the share of typed exercises stays as it was (a hint to makeExercise, which only chooses between exercises of the same kind); each device remembers the kind each grammar item was last shown with (itapp.lasttype.v1, never synced) and prefers another next time; a correct answer that schedules a review at box 3 or above moves it by up to 10% either way; an item with 4 or more lapses at box 3 or below is tricky, and starts again with its introduction and then the usual typed follow-up, Stats listing up to 15; Stats shows how many grammar items, cards and verbs fall due on each of the next 7 days; and the summary offers Another round. Boxes, intervals, promotion and every storage format are as before. New check: tools/test-sessions.js.
- **9.4** (2026-10-21): Cheaper cloud sync and sturdier progress, with no storage format changed: after an answer only the documents that changed (its record's shard, the year's daily log and meta) are built and compared, with every document compared on loading, after a backup or a reset and when the app is put away; a snapshot from the cloud merges only the documents new since the last one, record by record, without building the content again; and every progress record is checked as it is loaded, unpacked from the cloud, merged or read from a backup, a damaged one repaired or left out (loading a backup says how many). New checks: room for a record for every item, damaged records, writes limited to what changed, and the merge against 9.3's.
- **9.3** (2026-10-21): Faster on a phone, with nothing else changed: Look up and the glossary and Verbs filters search an index built once and redraw only their results once typing pauses, the Verbs list draws a verb's tables when its row is opened, the counts on Today, Units, Stats and You are kept until they can change, the word lists behind the typo rule and the other-words-with-this-meaning hint are built while the phone is idle, start-up saves progress only if loading changed it, and the installed copy checks for a new version on coming back at most every three hours.
- **9.2** (2026-10-21): No change in the app: its content is laid out one entry per line, so pull requests show content changes line by line, with new tools to confirm the content is unchanged, to edit and remove items, and to measure the variety of exercises in each unit.
- **9.1** (2026-10-21): A "What's new" card on Today after each update, automatic checks on GitHub for every pull request, a speed test, a tag for each version, and this changelog.
- **9.0** (2026-10-20): A new Vocab pack 7 of 1,000 upper-intermediate words, between packs 6 and 8; the old pack 7 becomes pack 8.
- **8.9** (2026-10-20): The Possessives unit grows by 80% (110 new exercises), with a new lesson section on longer phrases.
- **8.8** (2026-10-20): A new Possessives unit, with a full lesson, 131 exercises and four Wild card items.
- **8.7** (2026-10-20): A beginner Vocab pack 2 of 1,000 everyday phrases and words, the packs after it moving up one; English answers accept contractions (I'm = I am).
- **8.6** (2026-10-20): Units 1 and 2 roughly double, with five new kinds of exercise (Spot the mistake, Complete the endings, Reply, Sort the words, Match the pairs), and Listen and type and Make it plural in every unit.
- **8.5** (2026-10-20): A new vocab pack of 1,000 common words the packs had skipped (now Vocab pack 5), and the packs numbered from the most common words to the least.
- **8.4** (2026-10-18): Tapping a unit on Units opens it in place, and Today's Continue card follows the unit practised last.
- **8.3** (2026-10-18): Don't know and Check stay above the phone's keyboard on typed questions.
- **8.2** (2026-10-17): A speaker beside the Italian reads it aloud with the phone's own voice (Off, Normal or Slow).
- **8.1** (2026-10-17): Ripasso can be installed on Android from GitHub Pages: it works offline, offers updates on Today, follows the back button and reminds you to back up.
- **8.0** (2026-10-07): The Ripasso Italiano look: new name and logo, light and dark colours, a tab bar, the You screen and the "Lo sapevi?" card.
- **7.6** (2026-10-07): Commands and the subjunctive for the verbs in the Verbs section, and Units 12 and 14 expanded.
- **7.5** (2026-10-07): 206 new verbs and four new drills in the Verbs section, about 140 new exercises each and longer lessons in Units 3, 4 and 5, and an accuracy review.
- **7.4** (2026-10-07): A new vocab pack of 1,000 upper-intermediate words and idioms (now Vocab pack 8).
- **7.3** (2026-10-07): Clearer notes on 22 wrong options, and -ere verbs no longer all called regular.
